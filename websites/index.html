Before you start

Questions a serious buyer should ask.

Is this a template website?

No. The package is built around the agreed business, offer, visual direction and conversion path. Reusable technical patterns may be used where they improve reliability, but the client-facing design and structure are not sold as a one-template-for-every-business product.

Do I pay the full $3,500 upfront?

No. The direct checkout starts the project with a $1,750 deposit. That deposit is applied to the $3,500 total. The remaining $1,750 is due before final launch of the agreed core scope.

What do you need from me?

Usually your offer, business details, brand assets if you have them, useful photos/video, current website or domain details, and access to any agreed tools that need to be connected.

What happens if I need more than the core package?

Custom pages, advanced integrations or work outside the agreed core package are scoped and quoted separately before that additional work is done.

Who am I actually working with?

Revenue Pilots is founder-led. You communicate directly with Ostap, who is responsible for scope, creative direction and the build process rather than being passed through layers of account management.
