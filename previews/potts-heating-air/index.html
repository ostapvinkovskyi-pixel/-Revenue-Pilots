Make the website match the business.

The current public site still exposes obvious template contact data — including a 555 phone number and generic email — alongside the real business information. For a company with more than five decades of history, the first digital impression should feel just as established as the company itself.

Revenue OS layer

After the customer-facing path is fixed, the same setup can capture inquiries into one pipeline, notify the team, support booking where useful, and show what turned into real appointments — without forcing a complete software replacement on day one.
