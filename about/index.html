The person behind Revenue Pilots

I’m Ostap.I build creative for businesses that deserve to be noticed.

I started Revenue Pilots because I kept seeing the same problem: good businesses doing genuinely good work, but their advertising didn’t communicate that nearly as well as it could.

I’ve always been drawn to creative work. I make music, build ideas from scratch, and spend a lot of time thinking about what makes someone stop, feel something, and pay attention. Revenue Pilots became a way to apply that same creative thinking to business.

My goal isn’t to sell companies more marketing for the sake of marketing. It’s to understand what makes a business valuable, find the clearest and strongest way to communicate it, and turn that into creative people actually want to watch.

I care about the details, I communicate directly, and I’d rather make something genuinely useful than promise results I can’t control.

Revenue Pilots is still being built — intentionally. One business, one project, and one piece of great work at a time.

Ostap Vinkovskyi

Founder, Revenue Pilots

Make the business look as good online as the work it does in real life.
